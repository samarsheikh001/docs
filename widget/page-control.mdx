With page control on, the AI can see the page the visitor is on and use it for them: fill in a field, pick an option, press a button. Your site doesn't have to register anything. It works next to [page actions](/widget/page-actions), which stay the better choice when you have them.

It's on by default. You can switch it off for all your sites, or leave out one site.

## Turn it on or off

It's under **Chat widget → Behaviour → Let the AI use your website**. Every site with your snippet follows the switch within a minute; you don't change the snippet. Switch it off there: the AI stops being offered these actions at once, even on pages that are already open.

To leave one site out while it's on, add `data-page-control="off"` to that site's snippet:

```html
<script
  src="https://your-desk.example.com/widget.js"
  data-key="wk_…"
  data-page-control="off"
  defer
></script>
```

While it's off, nothing extra loads. With [`data-consent="required"`](/widget/consent-and-privacy), it loads only after `JunDesk.consent(true)`. In [`desk.yaml`](/code/files#desk-settings) it's `chat.pageControl`.

## What the AI can do

Page control adds five page actions. They go through the same steps as any other page action: the visitor sees each step in the chat, changes wait for their **Confirm**, and every run is in the conversation's AI actions.

| Action | What it does | Runs |
|---|---|---|
| `read_page` | Reads the page: its text, and the buttons, links and fields on it, each with a number | At once |
| `click` | Clicks an element by its number | After the visitor confirms |
| `type_text` | Types into a field by its number, replacing what was there | After the visitor confirms |
| `choose_option` | Picks an option in a dropdown by its number | After the visitor confirms |
| `scroll_page` | Scrolls up or down by one screen | At once |

A request like "set my company name to Acme Ltd" goes like this: the AI reads the page, proposes typing into the field (the card says *Company name field · Acme Ltd*, never a number), and after the visitor confirms, proposes the click on **Save**. It reads the page again whenever something changed.

If your page registers its own actions, the AI uses those first when one fits. To let clicks run without a confirm, or to send them to a person instead, set the action's risk in [`website-actions.yaml`](/ai/actions), like any other website action.

## The customer sees what it will do

While a click, typing or choice waits for the customer's **Confirm**, the element itself is ringed on the page in your brand colour, with a short label like *Type “Acme Ltd” · confirm in the chat*. If the chat covers it, the page scrolls it into view. When the customer confirms, a pointer moves from the chat to the element and clicks, then the highlight fades. Nothing on your page is changed by this: it's a layer on top that clicks pass through, and the AI never sees it.

## Always allow

By default each click, typing and choice asks for **Confirm**. The card also has **Always allow**: the customer confirms this step, and from then on page control acts without asking on your site, in their browser. Each step still shows in the chat and on the page. **Ask me first**, under the latest step, turns it back off.

You choose how it asks under **Chat widget → Behaviour → Before it clicks, types or chooses**:

| Choice | What customers get |
|---|---|
| **Ask, with Always allow** (default) | Each step asks for **Confirm**, and the card offers **Always allow** |
| **Ask every time** | Each step asks for **Confirm**; no **Always allow** |
| **Don't ask** | Steps run straight away; each still shows in the chat and on the page |

In [`desk.yaml`](/code/files#desk-settings) it's `chat.pageControlConfirm`: `allow-always`, `every-time` or `never`. A risk you set for `click`, `type_text` or `choose_option` in [`website-actions.yaml`](/ai/actions) wins over it.

## Across pages

The AI can follow a link or menu item to the page that has what the customer asked for, then go on there. "Turn my weekly emails off" can be: read the page, click **Email settings**, read the new page, choose **Off**, click **Save**. Each change still waits for the customer's Confirm, and the chat stays open across the page load.

This works for single-page apps and for links that load a whole new page: after a page load the chat opens again by itself and the run goes on. The AI never reuses an element's number after the page changed: it reads the new page first. If what the customer asked for isn't on the page they're on, the AI looks behind the likely links (their name or avatar, Account, Profile, Settings) before handing the chat to your team.

If the customer has your site open in several tabs, the steps run only in the tab they asked from.

## What it never sees or touches

- The Jun widget itself.
- Anything inside an element with `data-jun-private`.
- Password fields, hidden inputs, card fields (`autocomplete="cc-…"`) and one-time-code fields.

Put `data-jun-private` on anything else the AI shouldn't read or use, like an account number or an admin panel:

```html
<section data-jun-private>
  <p>Card on file: 4242 4242 4242 4242</p>
</section>
```

The AI is also told never to type passwords, card numbers or codes: it asks the visitor to type those themselves.

## Privacy

Normally the widget [never reads page text](/widget/debug-context). Page control changes that on the sites where you turn it on, and only when the AI calls `read_page` during a chat:

- The text is masked in the browser and again on the desk, with the same rules as the debug context: email addresses, card-like numbers, tokens and keys become placeholders. The page address loses its query values (campaign tags excepted).
- One read is at most 12,000 characters. Longer pages are cut, and the AI can scroll and read again.
- It can't run JavaScript on your page. It only reads, clicks, types, chooses and scrolls.
- Reads go into the conversation, so your team sees what the AI saw.

## Size and loading

The loader fetches `GET /widget-page-control.js` from your desk once, on sites with the attribute (about 12 KB gzipped). Allow your desk's origin in `script-src` if your site has a CSP. See [Widget options](/reference/widget-options#what-the-loader-loads).
