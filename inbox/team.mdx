Everyone on your desk signs in with their own passkey. There are no passwords and no email service: you add people by sending them an invite link, and they create a passkey when they open it.

## Roles

Each person has one of three roles.

| Role | Who | Can |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | The person who set up the desk with the setup token, or who created the workspace | Everything an admin can, plus invite admins, make agents admins and remove admins. Nobody can change or remove the owner. |
| **Admin** | People the owner invites or promotes | Change the workspace's settings, the AI, knowledge and the chat widget; invite and remove agents |
| **Agent** | People who answer chats | Work in the inbox and see the rest of the desk |

In more detail:

| | Owner | Admin | Agent |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Read, reply, write notes, assign, resolve, take over from and hand back to the AI | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Add tags to conversations, create and edit saved replies | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| File GitHub or Linear issues | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Start chats from the Visitors page, edit an anonymous visitor's name and email | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| See the Dashboard | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Their own notifications, passkeys and API tokens | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Rename and delete tags, manage topics | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Assignment, business hours, AI settings, issue trackers | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Change the agent (Instructions, Tasks, Actions, Widgets, Tests), knowledge and the chat widget | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Invite agents, change or remove agents | ✓ | ✓ | |
| Invite admins, change or remove admins | ✓ | | |

Agents see admin-only pages and settings read-only, without the buttons. The **Get started** checklist in the sidebar is for owners and admins only.

Your own role shows in **Settings → General** ("Owners and admins change workspace settings; agents answer chats.").

## Invite a teammate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Team">
    Open **Settings** from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar (or press `g` then `s`), then **Team**. Owners and admins see an **Invite people** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a role and create the link">
    Choose **Agent** or, if you're the owner, **Admin**. Press **Create invite link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the link">
    The link appears once, with **Copy**: "Send this link to the person you're inviting. It's shown only once." Send it however you like (chat, email). The desk doesn't send it for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They join">
    They open the link and see **Join \<your workspace\>**, "You've been invited as an agent." (or an admin). They enter their name and email and press **Create passkey and join**. Their browser or password manager creates a passkey, and they're signed in.

    Someone who already has an account on this desk (in another workspace) presses **Join as \<their name\>** if they're signed in, or **Sign in to join** under the form. The workspace is added to their workspace menu; no new passkey.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invite links work once and expire after 7 days. Pending invites are listed under **Pending invites** with who created them and when they expire; **Revoke** cancels one. Only a hash of each link is stored, so a lost link can't be shown again: create a new one.

Someone opening a used, revoked or expired link sees **This invite link is invalid, used, or expired.** Creating a new account with an email that's already on the desk gives **An account with that email already exists. Sign in with it to join.**

<Tip>
The invite link uses the address you created it from. If you use a [custom domain](/deploy/custom-domain), create invites from that address so passkeys are made for it: a passkey only works on the domain it was created for.
</Tip>

## Change a role or remove someone

The **Members** card lists everyone with their email. For people below your role, it shows a role menu and **Remove**:

- The owner can switch anyone between **agent** and **admin**, and remove admins and agents.
- Admins can manage agents only.
- Everyone else's role shows as a label.

**Remove** asks for confirmation ("They'll be signed out and lose access."). Removing someone takes them out of the workspace and stops notifications to their devices. If it was their only workspace, their account is deleted with its passkeys and sessions, so they're signed out everywhere at once. Their past messages stay in your conversations, and the Dashboard lists them as **Former teammate**.

## Passkeys

Each person manages their own passkeys in **Settings → Passkeys** (**Your passkeys**).

- **Add passkey** registers another one, for example on your phone or a second computer. Add at least one more so you can't get locked out.
- Each passkey shows when it was added and last used. **synced** means your passkey provider backs it up and syncs it across your devices (iCloud Keychain, Google Password Manager, 1Password and similar).
- **Remove** deletes a passkey. You can't remove your only passkey: add another one first.

Signing in is **Sign in with passkey** on the desk's sign-in page. New people can't sign up there; the page tells them to ask an admin for an invite link.

<Warning>
If an agent or admin loses every passkey, they can't recover access themselves. Remove them and send a new invite. The owner can recover with the setup token: see [First sign-in](/deploy/first-sign-in).
</Warning>

## Settings overview

Settings opens as a dialog over the page you were on. Each section has its own address (`/settings/<section>`), so you can link to it. The **Theme** switch at the bottom of its menu cycles between system, light and dark.

| Section | What's there | Who can change it |
|---|---|---|
| **General** | Your name and email, the workspace's name, your role, and **Delete workspace** | Rename: owners and admins. Delete: owners |
| **AI** | AI replies on or off, the provider and model, the monthly cap, and keeping the agent in git. See [Models](/ai/models). | Owners and admins |
| **Inbox** | [Assignment](/inbox/assignment), [Business hours](/inbox/business-hours), [Saved replies](/inbox/notes-and-replies#saved-replies), [Tags](/inbox/notes-and-replies#tags) and [Topics](/inbox/reports#topics) | Saved replies: everyone. The rest: owners and admins |
| **Team** | Members, invites and pending invites | Owners and admins |
| **Notifications** | Your own events, devices and sound. See [Notifications](/inbox/notifications). | Each person, for themselves |
| **Issue trackers** | GitHub and Linear. See [Issues](/inbox/issues). | Owners and admins |
| **Passkeys** | Your passkeys | Each person, for themselves |

The chat widget's look and install snippet are on the **Chat widget** page, not in Settings. Your API tokens for the `jun` CLI and your own AI are on the **MCP** page in the sidebar. See [MCP](/code/mcp). See [Appearance](/widget/appearance) and [Install](/widget/install).

You can also jump to any settings section from the command palette (`Ctrl` `K`): type its name, for example **Settings: Team**.

## Workspaces

One desk can run several websites or products. Each one is a **workspace** with its own chat widget and widget key, inbox, AI agent and settings, knowledge, team, API tokens and notifications. Nothing is shared between them except the desk itself: its address, its Worker secrets (such as `JUN_SECRET_*` keys, `GITHUB_TOKEN`) and the setup token.

- **Switch:** the workspace's name at the top of the sidebar opens a menu with your workspaces. Pick one to open it. The desk remembers it on this browser.
- **Create:** owners see **New workspace** in that menu. Give it a name; you're its owner, and it opens on [Get started](/get-started-checklist) to add its docs, set up its AI and install its widget on its website.
- **Rename:** **Settings → General → Workspace** (owners and admins). Customers see the name until the chat widget has its own.
- **Delete:** **Settings → General → Delete workspace** (owners). Type its name to confirm. It deletes the workspace's conversations, contacts, knowledge, AI agent, widget and API tokens for good, and its widget stops working on your website. Teammates who were only in that workspace lose their account. You can't delete your only workspace.

Teammates are per workspace: invite people from each workspace's **Settings → Team**. One person can be in several workspaces with one account and a different role in each. Notifications are per workspace too: turn them on in each workspace's **Settings → Notifications**. A notification opens its conversation in the right workspace.

An API token belongs to the workspace it was made in, so the `jun` CLI and your own AI over [MCP](/code/mcp) work on that workspace. Make a token in each workspace you want to reach.
